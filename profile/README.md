# The Realms of Asgard

**Building technology for community, resilience, and safer online spaces.**

The Realms of Asgard (TROA) builds and operates community platforms, game-server infrastructure, and digital tools focused on mental-health advocacy and safer online communities.

## What we build

- **Community platforms** — public web experiences, member services, support workflows, and internal operational tools.
- **Space Engineers infrastructure** — Torch plugins for reliability, observability, backups, economy, player safety, and server administration.
- **Community automation** — Discord integrations and services that help staff communicate, respond, and operate responsibly.

## Featured projects

- [TROA Econ+](https://github.com/troainc/TROA-Econ-Plus) — durable accounting, escrow, treasury policy, and integration APIs for Torch servers.
- [TROA Profiler+](https://github.com/troainc/TROA-Profiler-Plus) — headless performance intelligence and incident tooling for Space Engineers servers.
- [TROA Gridvault](https://github.com/troainc/TROA-Gridvault) — scheduled grid backups and safer recovery workflows.
- [TROA Hangar+](https://github.com/troainc/TROA-Hangar) — player grid storage, faction hangars, and market exchange.
- [Monitor+](https://github.com/troainc/TROADiscordSEMonitor-Releases) — Discord operations and player-facing tools for Torch servers.

## Engineering at TROA

Our systems span TypeScript and Next.js, C# and .NET/Torch, Go, PHP/Laravel, Python, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and self-hosted infrastructure. Across the stack, we prioritize auditability, privacy, safe recovery, operational clarity, and conservative failure handling.

Some repositories contain public releases and documentation while proprietary source and internal platforms remain private. Public issue trackers are the best place to report product-specific problems; please avoid posting credentials, player data, or other sensitive information.
