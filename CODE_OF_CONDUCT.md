# Community code of conduct

TROA is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and safety-conscious community.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and professionalism.
- Welcome constructive disagreement and focus criticism on ideas and outcomes.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and the confidentiality of operational information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption.
- Follow repository licenses, security guidance, and maintainer instructions.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact TROA through an official private support or moderation channel. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

These standards apply to TROA repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and related project spaces.
